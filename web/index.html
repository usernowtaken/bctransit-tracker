Add Bus Radar to your Home Screen

Open this website in Safari on your iPhone or iPad.

  1. Tap the Share button (on some versions, open the More menu first).
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen. You may need to scroll down.
  3. Turn on Open as Web App if shown, then tap Add.

Open Bus Radar from its new Home Screen icon. Live bus tracking needs an internet connection.
