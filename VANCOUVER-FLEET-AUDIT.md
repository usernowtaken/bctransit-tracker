- [Orion V retirement report](https://dailyhive.com/vancouver/translink-orion-v-bus-retirement) (fall 2026).

Cohort membership does not establish present activity, depot or actual paint. No live authenticated feed was inspected. The 2018–19 XDE60 batches use the green/blue RapidBus reference, independently of current route; 22001–22005 use conventional art and 22006–22015 use RapidBus art based on the documented mixed order; remaining batches use conventional reference art until individual livery data is available. The supplied HEV image represents both HEV generations; 2026 CNG buses have revised paint. Original uploads were individually named; ARBOC/Girardin and XD40/XD40 West Van are byte-identical pairs as supplied and have not been altered creatively. Missing matching Vancouver models stay placeholders.

Blue Bus aliases 80701–80706, 80901–80909, 81201–81217 are normalized only within those documented ranges. 16601–16605 are kept as reported provider IDs; equivalence to a physical four-digit number is not established. No general numeric suffix stripping.

Additional mixed-order evidence: [22001–22015 fleet entry](https://translink.fandom.com/wiki/Coast_Mountain_Bus_Company_22001-22015) and [Barp 2022 XDE60 unit photo archive](https://barp.ca/bus/bctransit/vancouver/newflyer/2022xde60/2022xde60.html). This is a reported delivery-livery split, not a live paint inspection.
